# SOURCES — The Zodiac Killer: The Ciphers, the Letters, and the Killer Nobody Could Identify

All factual claims in the narration and on-screen text were checked against the following sources in October 2026.

## Primary case archives

1. **The Zodiac Archive** — community-maintained archive of Zodiac letters, cards and ciphers (transcripts and scans).
   https://thezodiacarchive.com/ · cipher section: https://thezodiacarchive.com/ciphers
   Used for: letter dates and recipients (Aug 1 1969 ×3; Aug 4 1969 "This is the Zodiac speaking"; Oct 13 1969 shirt-swatch letter; Nov 8 1969 340; Apr 20 1970 Z-13; Jun 26 1970 Z-32 + Mt. Diablo map), cipher symbol counts, plaintext of the 408 and 340.

2. **FBI Vault — Zodiac Killer case files** (declassified).
   https://vault.fbi.gov/The%20Zodiac%20Killer
   Used for: the FBI's handling of the ciphers, case status statements.

## Cipher solutions

3. **Oranchak, David; Blake, Sam; Van Eycke, Jarl (2024). "The Solution of the Zodiac Killer's 340-Character Cipher."** Cryptologia (peer-reviewed write-up of the Dec 5, 2020 solution: period-19 irregular columnar transposition over a homophonic substitution).
   https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/01611194.2023.2274287
   Used for: the two-layer structure of the 340 and the method of solution.

4. **The Washington Post (Dec 11, 2020). "Zodiac Killer mystery cipher cracked 51 years after it was sent."**
   https://www.washingtonpost.com/crime-law/2020/12/11/zodiac-killer-340-cypher-solved/
   Used for: solver identities/backgrounds (Oranchak — Virginia; Blake — Australia; Van Eycke — Belgium), FBI confirmation, timing.

5. **Contemporary 1969 reporting on the Harden solution** (SF Chronicle, Aug 5 1969; AP wire summaries).
   Used for: Donald & Bettye Harden, Salinas; ~20 hours; the "I like killing…" plaintext; the final 18 characters never resolving.

## Case facts, victims, and status

6. **Zodiac Killer — confirmed attacks and victims** (cross-checked across the above archive, FBI Vault, and contemporary press summaries).
   Lake Herman Road, Benicia — Dec 20, 1968 (David Faraday, Betty Lou Jensen); Blue Rock Springs Park, Vallejo — Jul 4, 1969 (Darlene Ferrin; Michael Mageau survived); Lake Berryessa — Sep 27, 1969 (Cecelia Shepard; Bryan Hartnell survived; payphone call to Napa County Sheriff); Presidio Heights, San Francisco — Oct 11, 1969 (Paul Stine).
   Five confirmed deaths; two survivors; the killer's claim of 37 victims unsupported.

7. **FBI San Francisco public statements (Oct 2021)** on the "Case Breakers"/Gary Francis Poste claim — case remains open, no new information; claim rejected by investigators.
   Reported by AP/Chronicle, Oct 6, 2021.

8. **Suspect history** — Arthur Leigh Allen (searched 1991; never charged; died Aug 26, 1992; DNA from a confirmed-letter stamp reported to have excluded him — SFPD statements in 2002–2003 reporting and documentaries). Other named suspects (Gaikowski, Kane, Van Best Jr.) documented as theories without establishing evidence.

## Presentation standards

- Cipher grids, letters, newspaper pages, maps and the shirt swatch are **illustrative reconstructions**, labeled on screen. No archival photograph or police document is reproduced.
- Killer quotes (408/340 plaintexts, "This is the Zodiac speaking", "My name is") are quoted from the published solutions/letters via sources 1, 3, 4.
- Disputed items (Riverside 1966; the 1974 letters; claimed victims) are labeled as disputed on screen.
