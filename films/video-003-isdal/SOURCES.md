# SOURCES — The Isdal Woman (video-003)

All narration claims trace to the sources below. Where sources conflict, the film uses the
conservative reading and logs it in the claim ledger.

## Primary / official

1. **Bergen police case file 134/70** (as summarised in the 2016–2018 reinvestigation reporting and
   the Bergen police press materials referenced by the BBC). Cited for: case number, scene inventory,
   suitcases, hotel trail, aliases, the 1971 verdict of probable suicide, the 2016 reopening.
2. **Interpol circulation of composite sketches (1970–71)** — via police appeal reporting.

## Journalism & documentary

3. **BBC News, "Isdal Woman: The mystery death haunting Norway for 46 years"** (13 May 2017) —
   discovery, removed labels, suitcase contents, hotel behaviour, witness accounts.
   https://www.bbc.com/news/world-europe-39368113
4. **BBC World Service + NRK podcast, "Death in Ice Valley" (2018)** — hosts Marit Higraff & Neil
   McCarthy; eyewitness interviews; 2018 Bergen police press-conference materials; birthplace
   estimates; the case's modern reinvestigation.
5. **NRK broadcast coverage of the 2016 reopening** — six new sketches by artist Stephen Missal.

## Reference synthesis

6. **Wikipedia, "Isdal Woman"** (consolidated, with citations to Norwegian reporting) — used as the
   cross-check synthesis for: the scene inventory list, autopsy findings (Gades Institutt; 50–70
   Fenemal tablets; carbon monoxide; soot in airways; neck bruise; teeth/jaw retained), the alias
   list (Geneviève Lancier, Claudia Tielt, Claudia Nielsen, Alexia Zarne-Merchez, Vera Jarle, Fenella
   Lorck, Elisabeth Leenhouwer), notebook decoding, Penguin-missile proximity reporting, the 1991

   Colleen Fitzpatrick / DNA Doe Project involvement, burial details (5 Feb 1971, Møllendal, zinc
   coffin, sixteen officers). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isdal_Woman

## Claim ledger (as asserted in the film)

**CONFIRMED (multi-source, not contested):** discovery date/place; scene inventory items; all labels
removed; autopsy cause of death (barbiturate + CO); 50–70 Fenemal equivalent; soot in airways; teeth
and jaw removed and retained; suitcase contents incl. 500 DM in lining; sunglasses fingerprints
matched; Hotel Hordaheimen room 407; checkout 23 Nov 1970 in cash + taxi; ≥8 aliases, forms in
French/German, nationality always Belgian; street names false; saints' names pattern; notebook
decoded to dates/places; Interpol sketches; no missing-person match; 1971 verdict probable suicide;
burial details; 2016 reopening; NRK sketches; Death in Ice Valley podcast; isotope findings; mtDNA
H24; DNA Doe Project participation.

**DISPUTED (presented as disputed):** neck bruise — fall or blow; the 1991 taxi passenger; the 2005
two-men sighting; whether a second person was ever at the scene.

**NOT ASSERTED:** that she was a spy (film presents it as an unproven hypothesis with its
counter-evidence); that she was murdered (never asserted); that the deaths in missile zones prove
anything; the fisherman sighting is attributed to journalism, not police findings.
