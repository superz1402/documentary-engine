## Premise

Not a whodunit. A *who-was-she*. The film follows a single spine: a woman who systematically erased
her identity — and the exact questions that erasure left behind. The suspense comes from restraint:
each exhibit is presented, weighed, and honestly bounded. The three theories get equal procedural
respect and none is adopted.

## Visual language — ice-dossier

- Palette: fjord night `#0C141F`, cold paper `#E8EDF1`, glacier blue `#3E6C93`, evidence red `#A63D33`
  (stamps and warnings only), amber `#C9A227` (labels), ice `#B8CCD8`.
- Recurring motifs: rubber stamps (ink-turbulence filter), torn labels with a red cut mark, map pins,
  contour maps, snowfall on night scenes, the red crosshair-question mark (series motif).
- All visuals are **original schematic reconstructions** drawn in code (SVG). No AI-generated imagery,
  no archival photographs, no crime-scene depictions. Real documents are represented as stylised
  facsimiles and are never presented as authentic reproductions.
- Motion per MOTION-CRAFT.md: ease-out entrances, 2–4 frame staggers, holds on key ideas, no
  constant motion, reduced-motion respected on the web player.
